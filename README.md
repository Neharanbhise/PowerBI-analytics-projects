# PowerBI-analytics-projects

This repository contains interactive Power BI dashboards covering three primary domain areas: HR Analytics, Hospital Readmission Analysis, and IPL Sports Performance Tracking (2008–2025).

---

## 1. HR Analysis Dashboard

* **Objective:** Monitor workforce demographics, analyze employee attrition rates, and identify key organizational risk factors.
* **Key Metrics & Insights:**
* **Total Workforce:** 1,470 Employees | **Total Attrition:** 237 Employees | **Overall Attrition Rate:** 16.1%
* **Demographics:** Average employee age is **36.92 years** with an average tenure of **7.01 years**. Employee gender distribution stands at **60% Male** and **40% Female**.
* **Department & Education Drivers:** The highest attrition volume occurs in **Research & Development (56%)** and **Sales (39%)**. By education field, **Life Sciences (89)** and **Medical (63)** report the highest attrition counts.
* **Marital Status & Overtime:** **Single employees** experience the highest attrition rate at **25.5%**, compared to Married (12.5%) and Divorced (10.1%) workforce segments. Overtime work strongly correlates with higher attrition counts.
* **Satisfaction & Compensation:** Average environment satisfaction score is **2.72**. Employees who left had a significantly lower average monthly income compared to retained employees.

## 2. Hospital Readmission Analysis Report

* **Objective:** Track patient readmission risk factors, regional distributions, and demographic profiles to improve care delivery and post-discharge strategies.
* **Key Metrics & Insights:**
* **Risk & Readmissions:** Evaluates overall patient readmission risk scores against total age and prior readmission history.
* **Geographical Mapping:** Regional analysis covering **South, East, West, North, and Central** territories across global locations (including North America and Europe).
* **Demographic Breakdown:** Visualizes patient readmission distributions across age brackets, gender divisions, and prior admission counts.

## 3. IPL Analysis (2008–2025)
